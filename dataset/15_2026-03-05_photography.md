# Photography

I started learning photography using the camera I already have instead of buying new equipment. My first exercise was to take photographs of the same scene at different times of day. The changes in light affected the mood more than I expected. I am trying to learn composition before worrying about equipment. I also want to photograph places during future trips and organize the images by date and location. The hobby feels different from software because there is no obvious optimization target. A photograph does not have to be technically perfect to be interesting. I think that is useful for me because I often approach everything as a problem to optimize.
