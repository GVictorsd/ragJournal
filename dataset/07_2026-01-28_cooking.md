# Cooking

I tried making a vegetable rice dish from scratch and discovered that preparation takes more time than the actual cooking. I had all the ingredients but had not measured or chopped anything in advance, so the first part of the process felt chaotic. The final dish was good, although some vegetables became softer than intended. I wrote down the quantities and approximate cooking times so I can compare them with my next attempt. I also learned that cooking is easier when ingredients are prepared before the pan is heated. That sounds obvious, but it is similar to software development: preparing the inputs and understanding the sequence reduces mistakes during execution. I want to try the recipe again with a slightly different spice balance.
