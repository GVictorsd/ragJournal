# Reading

I finished a book about habits and wrote a summary immediately afterward. The idea that stayed with me was that changing the environment can be more reliable than depending on motivation. I applied that idea to technical learning by leaving my notebook open on the desk and keeping a small list of questions next to the computer. I also started making shorter reading notes. Instead of trying to capture every interesting sentence, I record the main argument, one example, and whether I think the idea is applicable to my own work. This should make the notes more useful for future retrieval. I am beginning to think of my personal notes as a searchable knowledge base rather than a collection of forgotten documents.
