# Weekend Reflection

This weekend was quieter than usual. I spent one morning cleaning my workspace and deleting old files. I found several abandoned tutorials and half-finished experiments. Instead of feeling guilty about them, I tried to understand why I stopped each one. Most were started because a technology looked interesting, not because I had a clear problem to solve. That observation connects with my current project-based learning approach. A project gives me a reason to continue when the novelty disappears. I kept a few useful experiments and archived the rest. My goal is to maintain a smaller set of active projects and finish them well enough to explain what I learned.
