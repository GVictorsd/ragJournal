# Food Experiment

I tried another cooking experiment and deliberately changed only one part of the recipe. This made it easier to understand the effect of the change. I increased the amount of roasted spices while keeping the ingredients and cooking time almost identical. The flavor improved, although the dish became slightly stronger than intended. I wrote down the result instead of relying on memory. This reminded me of controlled experiments in engineering: if too many variables change at once, it becomes difficult to know what caused the outcome. I would like to maintain a small recipe notebook with versions and observations rather than simply collecting recipes from the internet.
