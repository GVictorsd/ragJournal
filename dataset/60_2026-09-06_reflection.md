# Reflection

I looked back through the journal entries I have written this year and noticed that the topics are more connected than they first appear. Career planning led to AI learning, AI learning led to the journal project, the project exposed gaps in system design and cloud knowledge, and those gaps shaped later study sessions. Even hobbies such as photography and cooking have influenced how I think about experimentation and observation. This is exactly the kind of relationship I want the RAG system to retrieve. A useful assistant should help me discover patterns I did not explicitly write down, while still showing the entries that support those patterns.
