# Relationships

I called a family member today and we ended up talking for much longer than planned. The conversation reminded me that maintaining relationships requires attention even when there is no specific problem to solve. I have a habit of postponing calls when work becomes busy because I assume I can do them later. I want to change that by setting aside a little time each week for people who matter to me. I also realized that remembering details from previous conversations makes future conversations more meaningful. I might keep a lightweight list of birthdays, plans, and things people mentioned so I do not rely entirely on memory.
