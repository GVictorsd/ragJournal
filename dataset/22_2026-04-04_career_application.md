# Career Application

I spent today reviewing job descriptions and noticed that many software roles combine frontend, backend, cloud, and AI responsibilities. Rather than trying to present every skill equally, I am experimenting with focused versions of my resume. One version emphasizes frontend ownership and performance, another emphasizes full-stack development and cloud systems, and a third highlights AI application work. The underlying experience is the same, but the ordering and examples change according to the role. I think this is more accurate than claiming to be an expert in everything. I also want to keep measurable details where possible, such as the type of application, the technologies used, and the kind of problem I solved.
