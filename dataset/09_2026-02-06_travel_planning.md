# Travel Planning

I started planning a future weekend trip and deliberately avoided filling every hour with activities. In previous trips I often created detailed schedules and became disappointed when weather, traffic, or tiredness changed the plan. This time I want to reserve a few anchor activities and leave the rest flexible. I am particularly interested in visiting historical areas, trying local food, and walking through less touristy neighborhoods. I also created a small checklist for transportation, accommodation, weather, and emergency contacts. I think travel planning works best when the important logistics are certain but the experience itself has room for discovery.
