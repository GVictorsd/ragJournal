# Learning Strategy

I changed my weekly learning schedule so that each week has one primary topic and one supporting topic. The primary topic receives most of my focused study time and must produce a small artifact such as code, a diagram, or a written explanation. The supporting topic is lighter and can be explored through reading. This should reduce the feeling that I am constantly behind. I also want one review session each weekend where I summarize what I learned without looking at the original material. If I cannot explain the concept in my own words, I know I need another pass.
