# Programming Practice

I solved several SQL and data-structure problems today. I noticed that my first instinct is sometimes to write a query or algorithm immediately instead of clarifying constraints. When I slow down and identify the input size, ordering requirements, duplicates, and expected output, the solution often becomes simpler. I am trying to develop a habit of stating assumptions before coding. This also connects to system design interviews. Good engineering decisions depend on the problem definition, not just the implementation technique.
