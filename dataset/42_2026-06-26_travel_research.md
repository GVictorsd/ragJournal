# Travel Research

I researched a historical city I may visit later this year. Instead of only saving tourist attractions, I collected notes about neighborhoods, local transportation, food, museums, and the best times to visit different areas. I also recorded why each place interested me. This made me realize that a useful travel knowledge base needs more than names and links. Context matters. A future travel assistant could answer questions such as “Which places match my interest in history and quiet walking?” if my preferences and previous experiences are stored alongside destination information.
