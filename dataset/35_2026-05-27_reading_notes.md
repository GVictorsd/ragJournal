# Reading Notes

I returned to an older technical book and found that I understood some chapters differently than I did the first time. This made me think about the value of revisiting material after gaining practical experience. A concept that seemed abstract before can become obvious after encountering the same problem in a project. I added a “revisit later” section to my reading notes and linked it to projects where the concept might become relevant. I also started distinguishing between knowledge I can explain and knowledge I can actually apply. The second category takes longer to develop but is more useful during engineering work.
