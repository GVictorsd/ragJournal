# Music

I reorganized my music library and discovered several albums I had not heard in years. My listening habits seem to change depending on what I am doing. Instrumental music works better for focused technical work, while familiar songs are more enjoyable when I am cooking or traveling. I created a few broad playlists instead of trying to classify every song perfectly. The exercise made me think about recommendation systems because my own categories are contextual rather than fixed. A song can be appropriate for one activity and distracting for another. I would like to take more time to listen intentionally instead of always having something playing in the background.
