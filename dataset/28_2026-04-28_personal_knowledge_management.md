# Personal Knowledge Management

I reorganized my notes into a small hierarchy: projects, learning, personal, travel, finance, and ideas. I avoided creating too many categories because classification becomes a maintenance task of its own. I also decided that dates should remain part of the metadata even when the topic seems more important. A future question might be temporal, such as “What changed after I started studying cloud?” Without dates, that kind of query becomes much harder. This is another reason I like markdown for personal knowledge. It is readable without special software, easy to version, and straightforward to parse programmatically.
