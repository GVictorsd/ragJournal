# Career Change

Today I spent time thinking about career uncertainty and what skills remain valuable when tools change quickly. AI coding assistants can generate boilerplate and even suggest complete implementations, so simply knowing syntax seems less differentiated than before. I think deeper understanding of requirements, architecture, debugging, security, and trade-offs will remain important. I also want to become comfortable reviewing AI-generated code rather than competing with it at repetitive tasks. My learning plan is therefore shifting toward systems thinking and practical AI integration. I want to be able to build software with AI tools while still understanding why the system works.
