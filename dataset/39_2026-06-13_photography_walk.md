# Photography Walk

I went for a walk with the goal of taking photographs of ordinary objects rather than landmarks. I photographed doors, signs, shadows, street corners, and small details that I normally ignore. The exercise made the neighborhood feel unfamiliar in a good way. I noticed that looking for photographs changes how slowly I move through a place. I also experimented with taking notes about why I liked certain images. Sometimes it was the composition, but often it was the story I imagined behind the scene. I would like to combine photographs with journal entries eventually, although I need to think carefully about metadata and storage.
