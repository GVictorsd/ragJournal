# Morning Routine

My mornings have been inconsistent, especially on days when I work late the previous evening. I experimented with preparing everything the night before: clothes, water bottle, breakfast ingredients, and a short list of priorities. The small amount of preparation made getting started easier. I also decided not to check social media immediately after waking up. Instead, I spend the first few minutes reviewing the day's calendar and deciding what absolutely needs attention. This is not a rigid productivity system. I mainly want fewer decisions before I become fully alert. I noticed that when I start calmly, I am less likely to spend the first hour reacting to messages. I want to keep this routine for several weeks before deciding whether it is actually useful.
