# Travel and Food

I looked through my travel and restaurant notes together and noticed that food is one of the strongest parts of how I remember places. A particular dish can bring back a trip more quickly than a list of attractions. I started adding tags such as “quiet,” “street food,” “breakfast,” “historical,” and “scenic” to some notes. These tags could become useful filters in a personal retrieval system. For example, I might eventually ask for places I enjoyed that were quiet and had interesting local food. This is another example of why structured metadata can complement semantic search.
