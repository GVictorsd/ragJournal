# Interview Preparation

I started preparing for software engineering interviews by dividing the material into data structures, algorithms, SQL, system design, JavaScript, backend development, and behavioral examples. I am deliberately limiting the number of topics I study in one sitting because switching constantly makes it harder to notice gaps. For behavioral preparation, I am collecting stories about debugging difficult issues, improving performance, working with unclear requirements, and learning unfamiliar technologies. I want the stories to focus on what I actually did rather than using generic statements about teamwork. I also plan to practice explaining technical decisions aloud because knowing an answer silently is different from communicating it clearly.
