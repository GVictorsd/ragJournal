# Budget Review

I reviewed my monthly budget and compared it with the plan I created earlier in the year. The biggest difference was spending on learning resources and technology. Some of those purchases were useful, but I noticed that buying a course can create the illusion of progress. I want to evaluate learning purchases by whether I actually complete and apply the material. I also set aside a specific amount for travel so that travel spending does not compete directly with routine expenses. The budget is becoming more useful as a planning tool rather than a restriction. I plan to review it every month and adjust categories when my priorities change.
