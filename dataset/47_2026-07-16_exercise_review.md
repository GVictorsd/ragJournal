# Exercise Review

I reviewed my exercise routine after several months. The most sustainable part has been walking because it requires almost no setup. Strength training has been more variable because work deadlines sometimes interfere. Instead of abandoning it, I am reducing the minimum commitment on busy weeks. The goal is to preserve the habit even if the session is shorter. I also noticed that exercise affects mood and concentration more than the scale or any single performance measurement. I am going to continue focusing on consistency and recovery rather than chasing rapid changes.
