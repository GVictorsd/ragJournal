# Friendship

I met a friend for coffee and we talked about how differently our interests have changed over the last few years. It was interesting to compare our old plans with what we actually ended up doing. I realized that I often remember major events but forget the small decisions that led to them. Writing regular journal entries may help preserve that middle layer of memory. I also want the journal assistant to answer questions without inventing details. If the relevant information does not exist in the entries, it should say that clearly rather than producing a plausible story. That requirement will be an important evaluation case for the RAG project.
