# Food and Restaurants

I explored a few restaurants in my area and noticed that the places I enjoyed most were not necessarily the ones with the largest menus. I preferred restaurants with a small number of dishes that seemed carefully prepared. I wrote down what I ordered, what I liked, and whether I would return. I also started noticing how much atmosphere affects the experience. A meal is not only the food; service, noise, seating, and company all contribute. I want to keep these notes because they may become another useful retrieval dataset. A future query such as “Where did I enjoy a quiet dinner?” could require combining several details rather than matching a single keyword.
