# Resume Review

I reviewed my resume and noticed that some bullets described responsibilities but did not communicate outcomes. I rewrote several entries to explain the problem, what I built, and why the change mattered. I also reduced the number of technologies listed without context. A recruiter should be able to understand the type of systems I worked on without decoding a wall of acronyms. For AI-related work, I want to clearly distinguish production experience from personal experiments. Accuracy is more important than making the skill list look impressive.
