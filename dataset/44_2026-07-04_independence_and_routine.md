# Independence and Routine

I spent the holiday doing a mixture of learning and relaxing rather than following my normal work schedule. I cooked lunch, went for a long walk, and spent a couple of hours on the journal project. The relaxed schedule helped me notice that I enjoy technical work more when I am not constantly measuring productivity. I still want goals, but I do not want every activity to become a metric. The project progressed because I was curious about a retrieval problem, not because I had to complete a checklist. I want to preserve some of that curiosity in my regular routine.
