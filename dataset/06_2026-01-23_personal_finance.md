# Personal Finance

I reviewed my recent expenses and found that small online purchases were adding up. None of the purchases seemed unreasonable individually, but the total was larger than I expected. I divided my monthly budget into fixed expenses, savings, learning, travel, and discretionary spending. I also created a waiting rule for expensive technology purchases: if I still want the item after a week, I can evaluate it more carefully. The purpose is not to stop spending but to make the decision deliberate. I want to build a larger emergency fund because having financial flexibility would make career changes less stressful. I will review the categories at the end of each month rather than checking the budget obsessively every day.
