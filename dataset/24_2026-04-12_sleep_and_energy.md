# Sleep and Energy

I tracked my sleep and energy levels for several days without trying to change anything. The goal was to observe the baseline. I noticed that late-night technical work sometimes feels productive because I can focus without interruptions, but the cost appears the next morning. On days after shorter sleep, I spend more time rereading documentation and making small mistakes. I am going to experiment with ending focused work earlier and using the final part of the evening for lighter tasks such as reviewing notes. I do not want to turn sleep into another metric to obsess over. The useful question is whether a routine helps me maintain consistent energy across the week.
