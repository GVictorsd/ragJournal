# Fitness

I began a simple exercise routine with three strength sessions and several walking days each week. My goal is consistency rather than an aggressive target. I noticed that exercise affects my evening study sessions more than I expected. On days when I take a walk after work, I transition out of work mode more easily and can concentrate on learning afterward. I am also trying to avoid treating missed sessions as failures. If something interrupts the plan, I want to resume at the next reasonable opportunity instead of abandoning the whole week. I wrote down this approach because I tend to over-plan routines and then become frustrated when real life changes them.
